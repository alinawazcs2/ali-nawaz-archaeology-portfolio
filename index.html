Ancient South Asia

Indus Valley Civilization (IVC)

One of the world’s earliest urban civilizations

The Indus Valley Civilization flourished around 2600–1900 BCE in its Mature Harappan phase. Its cities and settlements are known for careful planning, drainage systems, craft production, long-distance trade, and distinctive seals and pottery.

Important sites include Mohenjo-daro and Harappa in present-day Pakistan, as well as Dholavira and Rakhigarhi in India.

Urban PlanningStreets, wells, and drainage systems.
Trade & CraftsBeads, pottery, seals, and exchange networks.
Indus ScriptSigns that remain undeciphered.
Key SitesMohenjo-daro, Harappa, Dholavira.
